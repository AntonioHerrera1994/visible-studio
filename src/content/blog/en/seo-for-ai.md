More people ask an AI before searching Google. If your business is missing from those answers, you lose customers who never reach your site.

## What AI tools look for
Clear answers, verifiable facts and trusted sources. A page that answers a question in its first paragraph is more likely to be cited.

## What you can do today
- Answer your customers' questions on your site, with clear headings.
- Keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere.
- Earn Google reviews and mentions on industry sites.
